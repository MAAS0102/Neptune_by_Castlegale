Features

Data Visualisation & Live Dashboards

See the flow across every tank in real time. Toggle effortlessly between your Plant Overview Dashboard and your own personal dashboards. Neptune turns your daily HMI and lab entries into interactive, customisable trend charts with clear target zones, making it easy to spot biological shifts, equipment wear or seasonal cost spikes before they become problems.

One Click Automated Reports

No Excel, no problem. Once we configure your custom templates, Neptune transforms your raw daily readings into polished Technical, Daily or Final EPA compliance reports automatically. Built in averaging, sum calculations and automated logic mean you never have to wrestle with broken spreadsheet formulae again.

Intelligent Alerts & Flagging

Never miss a critical threshold or compliance deadline. Set custom target boundaries and Emission Limit Values (ELVs) for any attribute across any tank. If a parameter drifts off target, Neptune immediately fires in app and email notifications to your chosen subscribers. Operators can review and acknowledge warnings with a single click right from the dashboard widget, creating a complete audit trail for shift handovers.

Bespoke Workflows & Logic Engine

Single data entry engineered for your exact plant. Log readings 68.4% faster using guided workflows organised either By Process (Inlet Sump, Balance Tank, DAF, Aeration, Clarifier) or By Attribute (COD, pH, Ammonia, Suspended Solids, Phosphorus). As you type, Neptune’s Advanced Logic Engine validates inputs against previous readings and computes F/M ratios and efficiencies automatically, while built in Sticky Notes and a Pocket Calculator keep shift handovers and on-the-fly maths in one workspace.

Secure Cloud & Document Vault

Store all of your EPA licences, lab certificates and historical records inside a centralised, encrypted Document Vault. Use Neptune’s instant Global Search and Data Explorer to pull up any tank, attribute or compliance file across multiple facilities in milliseconds, protected by end-to-end encryption, isolated databases and granular role based access.

In-App Support & Turnover Resilience

Built for real operators on any device. Whether your team is in the control room on desktop or walking the plant floor with a tablet or phone, Neptune keeps everyone connected. Self coaching workflows protect your plant from costly disruptions during staff turnover, and our integrated One Click Support Menu lets any operator flag an issue, ask a question or request a custom feature directly from their sidebar.
