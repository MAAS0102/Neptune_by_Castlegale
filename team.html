The Castlegale Story

Neptune was born on the plant floor, not in a boardroom.

Founded in Cork in 2019 by Finbarr Pyne and Declan Costigan, Castlegale Environmental has spent years advising Ireland’s leading dairy, pharmaceutical, medical device and industrial facilities on waste water compliance and process optimisation. Time and again, we saw brilliant plant teams held back by the same bottleneck: critical treatment data buried in fragile spreadsheets, paper clipboards and disconnected email threads.

Supported by the Local Enterprise Office (LEO) and Enterprise Ireland, and developed in collaboration with the CTO of an Enterprise Ireland HPSU-backed startup, we built Neptune to bridge the gap between environmental science and modern software. Proudly Irish-owned and developed in Cork, we cut through the red tape to give every facility a platform moulded to their exact plant, backed by real scientists you can call or bring on-site whenever you need us.
