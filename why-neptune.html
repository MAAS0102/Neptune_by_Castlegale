Why Neptune?

Clarity

Your ocean of plant data, simplified into a single source of truth.

Stop digging through multi tab spreadsheets to figure out how your plant performed yesterday. Neptune transforms raw HMI sensor readings and laboratory inputs into live, colour coded trend graphs across every tank and treatment stage. In your morning meeting you can see at a glance what’s on target and what isn’t, assign actions with shared sticky notes on the dashboard, and get on with your day. Hours spent finding and checking numbers go back into running your plant.

Control

Cost-saving, automatic process mastery across every shift.

Neptune’s Advanced Logic Engine eliminates manual “on-the-fly” maths with automatic calculations, including F/M ratios, dissolved oxygen (DO) efficiencies and chemical dosing trends. Daily visual trends help you spot subtle machinery wear before a breakdown occurs, fine tune aeration energy consumption, reduce chemical waste, and minimise sludge volume and disposal costs. Everyone sees a dashboard built around their role, so new starters get up to speed faster, and reminders and flags keep maintenance on schedule so small jobs don’t turn into big repair bills.

Compliance

Your ticket to a stressfree, audit ready plant 365 days a year.

EPA inspections are almost always unannounced, and evidence of active process control is what protects your waste water licence. Neptune automatically crosschecks every entered reading against your site’s regulatory Emission Limit Values (ELVs),and alerts you the moment a parameter starts to drift. Technical, Daily and Final reports are one click away, so your team’s time goes on the plant, not the paperwork. Certificates and reports sit in one secure, searchable vault, so you’re never hunting for a document with an inspector at the gate.

Case Studies

01 / 03

Pharmaceutical & Medical Device Manufacturing

Challenge: Strict EPA licensing requirements demanded airtight audit trails, rapid resolution of outside lab test results, and zero downtime during staff turnover.

Neptune impact: Centralised all HMI readings, lab analyses and EPA compliance certificates into a single encrypted hub. Automated 1-click Technical and Final reports, ensuring 100% readiness for unannounced audits regardless of staff leave schedules.

Dairy Processing & Cooperatives

Challenge: Complex and extensive spreadsheet setup, labour intensive daily data entry. Heavy laden with information & not enough transparency or visibility

Neptune impact: Deployed bespoke multi-plant dashboards with guided daily workflows across tanks and processes. Cut daily data entry time by over 68%, eliminated missed shift handover notes and automated daily COD and Final Outfall ELV tracking.

Industrial & Food Processing Facilities

Challenge: Rising chemical, energy and sludge disposal costs, paired with limited visibility into historical costs from unscheduled spikes.

Neptune impact: Implemented chemical delivery and usage tracking alongside real time biological process monitoring. Enabled early detection of pump and aerator variations prior to mechanical failure while optimising treatment dosing to reduce overall sludge volume.
